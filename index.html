Who We Are

A vital link between buyers and makers

A dynamic and fast growing sourcing company specialised in apparel fabric and sourcing, based in Sri Lanka, providing a vital link between customers & suppliers internationally and locally.

We work closely with manufacturing facilities of international companies, fully equipped with the most technologically advanced, efficient and competitive facilities for all processes. Our team has vast experience in the garment industry — from product development to garment manufacturing, and fabric dyeing to finishing.

We are linked with many state-of-the-art apparel manufacturers in Sri Lanka, India, Bangladesh and China, and undertake orders for lingerie, casual wear, pants, shirts and much more. The company has also diversified into exporting Sri Lankan spices, tea, coconut and coconut-based products to the international market.
