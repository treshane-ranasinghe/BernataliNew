Destinations

Twelve places that between them cover the coast, the highlands and the cultural triangle.

Galle

Discover the ancient charm of Galle, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Hikkaduwa

A vibrant coastal town renowned for its coral reefs and lively beaches, offering excellent snorkeling and diving.

Mirissa

A serene beach destination famous for whale watching and surfing, with stunning sunsets.

Anuradhapura

An ancient city and one of Sri Lanka's earliest capitals, showcasing well-preserved palaces, temples and stupas.

Ella

A picturesque hill town known for lush landscapes, tea plantations, the Nine Arches Bridge and Little Adam's Peak.

Kandy

Often referred to as the Hill Capital, Kandy was the last kingdom of Sri Lanka.

Nuwara Eliya

Known as "Little England", this hill station offers cool climates, colonial architecture and sprawling tea estates.

Yala

Home to Yala National Park, renowned for the highest density of leopards in the world.

Polonnaruwa

An ancient city of impressive stone carvings and ruins reflecting the grandeur of past civilizations.

Sigiriya

A rock fortress built by King Kashyapa in the 5th century, featuring elaborate gardens and frescoes.

Jaffna

A vibrant cultural hub in the north, known for its Tamil heritage, historic temples and scenic coast.

Trincomalee

On the eastern coast, with pristine beaches, ancient sites and one of the world's finest natural harbours.
