The Destination

A surfer's paradise

Weligama, located on the southern coast of Sri Lanka, is a stunning coastal town known for its golden beaches and crystal-clear waters. The town offers a serene atmosphere perfect for relaxation, with breathtaking sunsets and gentle waves ideal for surfing.

Beyond the beach, Weligama invites visitors to explore its rich local culture, delicious seafood, and warm, welcoming community. Whether you're seeking adventure or tranquility, Weligama promises an unforgettable experience amidst natural beauty and vibrant heritage.

5 fun things to do with your kids in Sri Lanka

5 fun things to do in Weligama besides surfing

Stilt fishermen at dawn, the fish market, Taprobane Island and the road east toward Mirissa.

A couples guide to beach hopping

Weligama, Mirissa and Unawatuna sit within a short drive of one another — one bay per day is the right pace.

Getting There

Find Weligama

  • About 2.5 hours from Colombo on the southern expressway
  • Coastal railway line stops at Weligama station
  • Mirissa and Unawatuna are both a short tuk-tuk ride away
